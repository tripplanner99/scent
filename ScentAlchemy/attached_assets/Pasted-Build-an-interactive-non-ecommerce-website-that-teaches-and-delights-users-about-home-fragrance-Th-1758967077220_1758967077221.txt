Duality rules (must-follow)

Every content entity (room scene, scent card, mascot message, illustration, animation) must have two variants (A and B). The CMS data model must store variantA and variantB for each field that changes (colors, copy, images, animations, ambience sound, scent-intensity mapping).

Provide a global Duality Switcher (top-right) that toggles the whole site between the two states. Additionally, allow per-section duality overrides (e.g., set "Mumbai vs New York" specifically for the living room).

Duality presets: ship with first set: Day | Night, Mumbai | New York, Traditional | Contemporary, Minimalist | Maximalist. Allow editors to add custom dual-pairs.

Core interactive features (detailed)

Interactive Home Map / Floor Plan

Click or tap a room to open its interactive canvas. Each room loads the room’s dual variants.

Rooms are drop zones for scent assets (diffusers, candles, incense, eau de parfum vials). Dropping an asset triggers a visual scent trail, mood-change, and an educational tooltip (“Why this suits night/day or Mumbai/New York”).

Doodle Board (per room + global)

Users can doodle shapes, draw scent trails, or stamp icons (flower, spice, wood). The system interprets simple doodles into fragrance moods (star → night, sun → day).

Doodles animate: a swirl becomes incense smoke, a scribbled sun brightens the scene. Doodles can be saved to a “postcard” and shared (no checkout).

Drag & Drop palette

Left palette: scent assets (Reed Diffuser, Incense bundle, Eau de Parfum sample, Roll-on), each with dual-state visuals + mini audio cue. Drag onto rooms to see immediate ambience change.

Each asset has range controls (intensity slider) and physics-based placement (snap to furniture or hang in air).

Duality selector & contextual transform

Switching duality morphs colors, lighting, copy, scent suggestions, background sound and micro-animations smoothly (e.g., sun → moon, monsoon rain overlay → subway hum for Mumbai→NY).

Playful micro-interactions

Hover → micro-animation (candle flicker, reed ripple).

Click → short guided narration by a dual-personality mascot (Sun/Moon spirit) giving tips.

Subtle soundscape changes with dual variant.
